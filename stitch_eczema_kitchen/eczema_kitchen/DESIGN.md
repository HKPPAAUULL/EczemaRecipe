## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a gentle, low-stimulation, and reassuring sanctuary for individuals managing atopic dermatitis, eczema, and cutaneous sensitivities across Hong Kong and Taiwan. The visual tone balances holistic dietary editorial sophistication with soothing clinical clarity. Rather than confronting the user with high-energy digital cues or stark hospital sterility, the experience evokes the quiet solace of an herbal apothecary, an editorial wellness quarterly, and an empathetic kitchen table.

Every surface, line height, and color transition is deliberately engineered to reduce sensory fatigue. Ample whitespace breathes physical calm into the layout, while deliberate typographic choices communicate quiet medical authority and emotional warmth. The overarching design direction combines warm, tactile paper-like foundations with disciplined, airy modern layouts, delivering clarity, trust, and gentle guidance.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in muted herbal botany and warm natural fibers, eliminating harsh optical glare.

- **Primary (`#5A7C6D` / `#6B9080` range)**: Soft sage and dried eucalyptus. Conveys holistic healing, cellular repair, and calm equilibrium. Used for primary interactive triggers, vital states, and editorial accents.
- **Secondary (`#64746B`)**: Muted slate moss. Serves as supporting graphic framing, subheadings, and secondary actions.
- **Background & Canvas (`#F8F5EF`)**: Oatmeal linen off-white. Replaces cold digital whites with a restful, warm ground that reduces eye strain.
- **Surface Alternates (`#F0F3F1`, `#EAF4F4`)**: Soft steamed-mint and ivory tints used for recessed panels, recipe step cards, and subtle contrast zones.
- **Text & Structure**: 
  - Primary Typography: Soft charcoal (`#2F3A35`), providing deep legibility without the jarring contrast of pure pitch black.
  - Secondary Typography: Muted slate green (`#64746B`), maintaining graceful hierarchy.
  - Borders & Hairlines: Warm oatmeal taupe (`#E3DDD3`), grounding cards and dividers with tactile paper seams.

### Suitability Triage System
Dietary assessment states rely on an empathetic trichromatic scale pairing tone, iconography, and text:
- **一般適合 (Generally Suitable)**: Forest emerald (`#4E8752`) against a soothing pale mist background (`#E8F5E9`).
- **因人而異 (Varies by Individual)**: Warm toasted ochre (`#D9822B`) against a gentle sunlit wheat container (`#FEF3E2`).
- **常見誘發 (Common Flare Trigger)**: Muted terracotta rose (`#C85A54`) grounded in a pale clay tint (`#FCE8E6`), deliberately rejecting alarming synthetic reds.

## Typography

Typography bridges timeless literary credibility with effortless digital scanning. 

- **Headlines (`Newsreader`)**: Literary serifs infuse dietary narratives, nutritional guides, and recipe names with human depth, intellectual poise, and artisanal craftsmanship. It is deployed for introspective moments, section headings, and article titles.
- **Body & Labels (`Noto Sans` / `Noto Sans TC`)**: Standardized for all continuous reading, UI elements, data tables, and allergen warnings. Traditional Chinese glyphs are spaced with generous horizontal tracking and airy line-height scales (`1.75` to `2.0` multiplier) to prevent visual crowding in dense logographic text.

All health disclaimers are set with deliberate, unhurried line heights to respect readability while preserving medical sobriety.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an airy fluid grid system centered on a max-width container of `1200px` for desktop and `768px` for focused reading and tablet experiences. 

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column layout with `2.5rem` outer canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content blocks celebrate negative space, letting recipe summaries and clinical annotations sit alongside serene photography.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Recipe metadata shifts gracefully from horizontal stat rows into vertically flowing rhythm blocks.

Spacing tokens dictate clear, deliberate breathing intervals between allergen groups, scientific ingredient citations, and culinary preparation steps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids intense drop shadows, unnatural artificial glow, or high-gloss skeuomorphism. Instead, the interface adopts a tactile paper-layering approach:

- **Base Canvas**: Oatmeal background (`#F8F5EF`) serving as natural, unbleached linen.
- **Surface Tier (Cards & Containers)**: Crisp chalk or soft mint (`#FFFFFF` or `#F0F3F1`) lifted by an ultra-diffused atmospheric shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(47, 58, 53, 0.05)`. The shadow is tinted with the primary charcoal green, ensuring harmony with the canvas rather than an aloof synthetic grey.
- **Interactive Hover & Modals**: Soft elevated lift using `0 10px 30px -4px rgba(47, 58, 53, 0.08)`.
- **Low-Contrast Seams**: Outlines in warm taupe (`#E3DDD3`) replace harsh bounding borders, creating a pressed-paper feel that frames content cleanly without inducing visual tension.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy prioritizes organic curves, avoiding both clinically sharp 90-degree corners and toy-like hyper-roundedness:

- **Standard Cards & Structural Panels**: `16px` (`rounded-lg` under level 2 tokens), creating a friendly container for recipes and health logs.
- **Form Fields & Inputs**: `12px` corner rounding for comfortable, tactile interaction on both touchscreens and pointer devices.
- **Chips, Badges, and Allergen Pills**: Full capsule shape (`rounded-full` / `9999px`), evoking smooth sea stones or gentle herbal pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid sage green (`#5A7C6D`) background, ivory text, 12px rounded corners, 48px standard touch target height. Subtle shift to `#4A695C` on hover.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Warm taupe outline (`#E3DDD3`), soft charcoal text (`#2F3A35`), transparent background transitioning to `#F0F3F1` on hover.

### Allergen Tags & Filter Badges
- Capsule pills (`rounded-full`), framed with a crisp `1px` outline in `#E3DDD3`, text in `#64746B`, and a clean `#FFFFFF` ground.
- Standard allergens include: 蛋 (Egg), 奶 (Dairy), 花生 (Peanut), 堅果 (Tree Nuts), 大豆 (Soy), 小麥 (Wheat), 魚 (Fish), 甲殼類 (Crustaceans), 芝麻 (Sesame).
- Selected state fills with subtle sage mist (`#EAF4F4`) and transitions border and text to `#5A7C6D`.

### Suitability Triage Cards & Banners
- **Banner Architecture**: Structured card with a soft-tint background, a left-aligned status badge with embedded indicator icon, bold verdict, and clinical rationale.
- **Tokens**:
  - *一般適合*: Background `#E8F5E9`, text and icon `#4E8752`, border `#D4EAD6`.
  - *因人而異*: Background `#FEF3E2`, text and icon `#D9822B`, border `#FADDBB`.
  - *常見誘發*: Background `#FCE8E6`, text and icon `#C85A54`, border `#F5C7C3`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs & Search**: 12px border radius, `#FFFFFF` interior, `#E3DDD3` default border, transitioning to `#5A7C6D` on focus with an ambient halo ring (`rgba(90, 124, 109, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Smooth rounded-sm (4px) for checkboxes and pill circles for radios, filled with `#5A7C6D` when checked.

### Content Cards
- `16px` border radius, `#FFFFFF` or `#F0F3F1` surface, subtle ambient drop shadow (`0 4px 20px -2px rgba(47, 58, 53, 0.05)`), bounded by `#E3DDD3` hairline stroke.
- Generous internal padding of `1.5rem` (`space-lg`), separating dish photography, title, allergen summary, and suitability rating.

### Disclaimer Footer
- Anchored to every layout bottom with a quiet border-top in `#E3DDD3`.
- Set in `disclaimer` typography (`12px`, `#64746B`, leading `20px`):
  > 「本站資訊僅供參考，不能取代醫生或營養師意見。」
